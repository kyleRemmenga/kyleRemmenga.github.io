Stars Without Number character generator

A Python generator for the Stars Without Number tabletop RPG, built to make NPCs more interesting than what I was coming up with on my own. It follows the core rulebook's character creation steps and the Codex of the Black Sun magic system: 14 classes, 44 backgrounds, 52 foci, four spell traditions, six psychic disciplines, and an equipment system across tech levels 0 through 5. You can generate a batch of options to one file and pick your favorite.
