About

I grew up in Colorado and went to Colorado Mesa University in Grand Junction for my undergrad. I'm now in Littleton, finishing a master's in computer science at the Colorado School of Mines.

Most of what I do day to day is retrieval-augmented generation and information extraction — parsing web pages, PDFs, and old documents, then making them answerable. Before that I trained transformer models in PyTorch and spent a summer working on a variation of a cryptographic hashing algorithm.

A retrieval pipeline on its own isn't much use to anyone, so a lot of my time goes to the software around it — the interfaces, the features, the things that turn a working pipeline into something people can actually sit down and use. I also build small tools for myself whenever something in my own workflow is annoying enough to fix.

What I've found I enjoy most is the measuring part: figuring out whether a change actually made the system better, rather than trusting that it did.
